Real World Asset tokenization converts physical assets like real estate, gold, and bonds into blockchain-based tokens, enabling fractional ownership and 24/7 trading. The piece notes regulatory fragmentation and smart contract security as challenges even as institutions adopt tokenized assets for liquidity and programmatic finance.
